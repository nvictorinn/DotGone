# DotGone Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

**DotGone does not collect, store or send any personal data.**

## What stays on your Mac

DotGone works entirely on your Mac. Your settings, the history of cleans (drive names and the
names of the files removed) and its statistics are stored only on your Mac, in DotGone's own
folders. They never leave it. Uninstalling DotGone and deleting its folders removes them.

DotGone has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## Update checks

To find new versions, DotGone downloads a small file (`appcast.xml`) from GitHub once a day, or
when you choose "Check for Updates…". You can turn automatic checks off in Settings › General.
This request contains no information about you or your Mac beyond what any web request carries
(such as your IP address), and DotGone does not send a system profile. GitHub handles it under
the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Notifications

If you allow them, notifications are shown by macOS on your Mac only.

## Changes

Any change to this policy will be published here, with a new date.
